`index_clusters.json`.

- Candidate decision timestamps `T` are the interval-end timestamps at every

- One base state at `T` produces linked rows for horizons `{1h, 4h}` and, once
  the twin is available, label families `{fixed, twin}`. Direction is an outcome,
  never a sampling criterion.
- The row enters the risk set using information at or before `T`. It is not

- Require a D-022 close exactly at `T`, the precondition endpoints below, and the

- A row whose full future wall-clock window `(T, T+H]` is not observable on the

  reported.

## Precondition impulse rule (closes the open decision)

EXP-004 is on the precondition clock. At candidate `T`, define

```text
i_T = abs(log(P_T / P_{T-15m}))
```

using D-022 closes exactly at both interval-end timestamps. The row is
precondition-eligible iff `i_T < log(1.005)`, an absolute trailing 15-minute move
strictly below 0.50%. Equality is excluded. A missing endpoint makes the row
`MISSING_PRECONDITION`, never imputed. The 0.50% threshold is one quarter of the
fixed 2% barrier and is applied unchanged across horizons, directions, label
families, splits, and regimes.

This rule uses price only so the fixed and twin families retain common support.
It does not claim that all ignition is price-led. Ignition/continuation rows are
outside EXP-004 and remain unauthorized.

## Exact outcome and competing risks

For label family `L`, freeze the barrier at `T`:

```text
b_fixed(T) = 0.02
b_twin(T)  = x_T
upper      = P_T * (1 + b_L(T))
lower      = P_T * (1 - b_L(T))
```

Scan D-022 minute bars with interval ends in `(T, T+H]` in timestamp order.
The single categorical outcome is:

- `UP`: the upper barrier is touched first;
- `DOWN`: the lower barrier is touched first;
- `NONE`: neither barrier is touched in the fully observed window;
- `AMBIGUOUS`: the first touching bar reaches both barriers and ordering is not
  observable;
- `CENSORED_GAP`: one or more expected D-022 minutes in the outcome window is
  absent.

Record the first touching bar's interval-end timestamp for `UP` and `DOWN`.
`AMBIGUOUS` and `CENSORED_GAP` are excluded from scores and reported; neither is
silently a negative.

For primary direction `d`, the exact binary target is
`Y_{d,H,L}=1` iff the categorical first cause is `d`. `NONE` and the opposite
direction are `0`: the opposite barrier is an explicit competing event, not a
censored observation. Thus the two directional probabilities for a horizon are
joint cumulative-incidence probabilities and must satisfy
`p_up + p_down <= 1`; the residual is `p_none`. Separate unconstrained binary
scores that can violate this identity are prohibited.

## Volatility-normalized twin (closes D-026 estimator)

On the D-022 index:

```text
r_u = log(P_u / P_{u-1})
sigma_T = sqrt(sum(r_u^2 on (T-1440m, T]))
x_T = kappa * sigma_T
```

A return exists only for closes exactly one minute apart. Require at least 720
finite one-minute returns and `0 < x_T < 1`; otherwise the linked fixed/twin
state is missing from common-support comparison. No annualization, demeaning,
forward fill, or path update is allowed.

Lock `kappa` on precondition-eligible D-023 development hourly timestamps with
defined `sigma_T`, before reading any outcome:

```text
kappa = 0.02 / median(sigma_T)
```

Store `kappa` to six decimal places and apply it unchanged to validation and
test. The fixed and twin outcomes use the same timestamps, precondition filter,
first-passage scan, competing-risk rule, and missingness mask for comparative
metrics. Fixed ±2% remains the scaffolding label; D-026 still blocks a PASS
headline without the twin companion family.

## Dependence and weighting

- Each scoreable hourly risk-set row has base weight 1 for probability metrics.
  No case-control sampling, inverse event-rate weights, duration weights,
  anchor-count weights, or class balancing.
- The linked horizons, directions, label families, and model rungs at one `T`
  are never independent replicates.
- Positive fixed-barrier rows map to their D-014 cluster without using that
  mapping for selection. Twin positives are clustered by the same D-014 rule
  on their eligible positive anchors. Mixed clusters are retained: a cluster
  contributes once to each direction it actually contains, and never once per
  positive anchor. A direction x horizon cluster is eligible for alert recall
  only when at least one scoreable hourly risk row with that first cause maps to
  it.
- Probability/calibration metrics describe the prospective hourly state
  population. Alert recall and lead time use one equal-weight contribution per
  direction x horizon x event cluster. Alert precision uses one equal-weight
  contribution per alert episode. Report timestamp rows, alert episodes, and
  independent clusters together.
- Confidence intervals use one family-wide UTC-week block draw of decision
  timestamps, `B=1000`, `numpy.random.default_rng(20250825)`, SE `ddof=1`.
  A sampled week carries every linked horizon, direction, fixed/twin, and model
  row. Event-cluster contributions are assigned to the UTC week of cluster
  start and carried whole; alert episodes are assigned to their onset week and
  carried whole. A sampled week's multiplicity applies to all linked objects.

## Evaluation metrics (for a later P6 commission)

Spearman against event-only rows is not an evaluation metric. Any later score
must emit joint probabilities `(p_up, p_down, p_none)` per horizon and report,
for fixed and twin families and for validation, 2025 test, and 2026-01..07 test
separately:

1. **Calibration / probability score:** per-cell Brier score; observed event
   rate and mean predicted probability; calibration intercept and slope from
   `Y ~ 1 + logit(p)` with `p` clipped only for this diagnostic to
   `[1e-6, 1-1e-6]`. Undefined diagnostics remain undefined.
2. **Lift over the preceding rung:** per-cell relative Brier skill
   `1 - BS_model / BS_preceding`. M0's preceding comparator is the frozen
   development cause rate; M1's is M0 on identical rows. The family statistic
   is the equal-weight mean of the four primary-cell skills. No cluster-size
   weighting.
3. **Fixed alert budget:** freeze each cell's threshold at the development
   99th percentile of its eligible predicted probabilities
   (`numpy.quantile(..., 0.99, method="higher")`) and alert only when `p` is
   strictly greater than that threshold. Apply it unchanged OOS and report the
   realized alert-time share. Consecutive hourly alerts separated by no more
   than one hour form one alert episode; missing/ineligible rows close it.
4. **Episode precision:** share of alert episodes containing at least one
   target-direction positive row.
5. **Event recall:** share of eligible direction x horizon event clusters with
   at least one linked alert row. Each cluster weighs 1.
6. **Lead time:** for each recalled cluster, time from its earliest linked alert
   row to the first subsequent target-direction passage in that cluster;
   report the median over clusters.
7. **Accounting:** scoreable/censored/ambiguous timestamp counts, alert-episode
   count, event-cluster count, and the required direction, horizon, volatility,
   session, news-protocol, and mixed/one-way slices. Slices with fewer than 30
   clusters are reported but not interpreted.

Minimum effect worth carrying forward from a later authorized run: family
relative Brier skill at least 1%, episode precision at least 2x the scoreable
base event rate at the fixed budget, event-cluster recall at least 10%, and
median lead time at least 15 minutes (1h) / 60 minutes (4h). These are
materiality gates, not results. A later verdict must state stability across
validation and both contiguous test periods; P5 inspects none of them.
